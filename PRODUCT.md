# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the project manager responsible for SplitIt delivery. They use the workspace during active project work to identify what needs attention without reconstructing status across tools.

## Product Purpose

Turn trustworthy project data into explicit next actions. A successful session leaves the PM knowing what requires attention, why it matters, and what to do next.

## Positioning

Project truth to action.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, decisive. The product should feel as restrained and dependable as Linear: fast, familiar, and focused on the work rather than the interface.

## Anti-references

Generic AI dashboard. Avoid gradients, chat gimmicks, excessive cards, decorative metrics, and visual effects that compete with project information.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action unmistakable.
- Show source-backed facts before interpretation or recommendation.
- Keep project utilities secondary to the task in progress.
- Use familiar controls and remove choices that do not improve the outcome.
- Require human approval for consequential changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, semantic structure, and reduced motion. Never rely on color alone to communicate state.
